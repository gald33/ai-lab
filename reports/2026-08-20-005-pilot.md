# 005 pilot — does the market have headroom?

**Date:** 2026-08-20 · **Status:** pilot run, gate passed · **Cost:** none (no model calls)
**Records:** [`pilot_sweep.json`](../experiments/005-deliberation-protocol/results/pilot_sweep.json) (27 configurations × 40 worlds, the pre-registered sweep) ·
[`pilot_confirmation.json`](../experiments/005-deliberation-protocol/results/pilot_confirmation.json) (the 6 accepted configurations × 200 worlds)
**Pre-registration:** [`PREREGISTRATION.md`](../experiments/005-deliberation-protocol/PREREGISTRATION.md), frozen in the commit before the run

## What was gated on what

005 tests whether a content-free **deliberation protocol** improves coordination
with the hint held fixed. Before any of that can be measured, the market has to
have headroom: if unguided agents agree immediately, or never, all four cells
read the same and the money is wasted.

So the pilot runs **unguided worlds only** — no protocol, no placebo, no hint,
no manipulation of any kind — over a grid fixed in `pilot_experiment.py` before
the run, and accepts a configuration only if all four pre-registered criteria
hold.

The four criteria exist because the first draft of this design said "visible
spread in time-to-coordination", which is a judgement call made after seeing the
data. They are now arithmetic:

| # | criterion | test |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | not hopeless, not trivial | coordination rate at τ=0.10 in `[0.15, 0.60]` |
| P2 | not instantaneous | ≤40% of coordinating worlds agree at round ≤1 |
| P3 | not pinned at the ceiling | ≤40% agree in the final quintile of the budget |
| P4 | genuine spread | IQR of rounds-to-coordination ≥2 **and** ≥8 worlds coordinate |

## What the agents are

Scripted, and that is a limitation rather than a stand-in. Each agent holds a
private noisy signal about the market's answer, sees a **sample of what other
agents actually submitted** — never a broadcast, never a harness-computed
average — and moves partway toward it while staying anchored to its own signal.

Three knobs: `sigma` (signal noise), `width` (how many others each agent sees),
`anchor` (how much weight it keeps on its own view). The grid is the full
factorial `3 × 3 × 3`, spanning near-pure averaging to stubborn, one neighbour
to half the population.

002's Tier 3 established that scripted traders have no beliefs about other
agents, so they **cannot be given a deliberation protocol at all** — the four
cells of 005 would be byte-identical. What a script can establish is that the
market has headroom. That is the entire claim of this report.

## Result: the gate passes, narrowly and for one reason

**27 configurations evaluated. 6 accepted. 0 harness failures.**

| configuration | rate | n coord | inst | late | IQR | median | median error |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `s0.15 w1 a0.30` | 0.350 | 14 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 8.0 | 8 | 0.070 |
| `s0.15 w2 a0.30` | 0.425 | 17 | 0.06 | 0.06 | 2.0 | 3 | 0.069 |
| `s0.15 w4 a0.30` | 0.375 | 15 | 0.40 | 0.00 | 3.0 | 2 | 0.059 |
| `s0.30 w1 a0.15` | 0.250 | 10 | 0.00 | 0.10 | 3.5 | 12 | 0.121 |
| `s0.30 w2 a0.15` | 0.325 | 13 | 0.00 | 0.08 | 4.0 | 6 | 0.159 |
| `s0.30 w4 a0.15` | 0.375 | 15 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 3.0 | 4 | 0.113 |

The 21 rejected configurations fail in a single, informative pattern: **`anchor`
is the only knob with headroom on it.** All nine `anchor=0.05`
configurations coordinate at 0.300 to 1.000, six of them at exactly 1.000 —
near-pure averaging converges. At `anchor=0.30` with any real noise, the rate is 0.000 to
0.025 — agents pinned to their own signal never converge. The band lives on a
thin ridge between them, and `sigma` and `width` mostly move where the ridge is,
not whether it exists.

Prediction 4 was pre-registered as "the most likely single outcome is that the
pilot fails P1 **upward**". Across the grid it mostly does — 11 of 27
configurations coordinate at ≥0.70 — but the ridge exists and the gate passes.

## Three things worth more than the pass

### 1. The threshold is doing most of the work

The sensitivity curve, required by the pre-registration and reported for the
configuration closest to band centre:

| τ | 0.02 | 0.05 | **0.10** | 0.15 | 0.20 | 0.30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| rate | 0.000 | 0.000 | **0.375** | 0.825 | 1.000 | 1.000 |

Every accepted configuration has this shape. **Nothing coordinates at τ=0.05 and
almost everything coordinates at τ=0.20.** The band is not a property of the
market alone; it is a property of the market *and* the choice of 0.10.

This is the single largest threat to 005 and it is a threat the pre-registration
created the means to see. Two readings, and this pilot cannot distinguish them:
the population genuinely clusters at a characteristic dispersion near 0.1, or
τ=0.10 is an arbitrary cut through a smooth distribution and the "coordination
rate" is a quantile in disguise. **A reviewer should push here first.**

### 2. Forty worlds is too few to evaluate the criteria themselves

Re-running the 6 accepted configurations at 200 worlds, **one flips to
rejected**: `s0.15 w4 a0.30` fails P2 — its instant share was 0.40 at n=40,
exactly on the boundary, and lands above it at n=200.

| configuration | rate @40 | rate @200 | verdict @200 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `s0.15 w1 a0.30` | 0.350 | 0.325 | accepted |
| `s0.15 w2 a0.30` | 0.425 | 0.405 | accepted |
| `s0.15 w4 a0.30` | 0.375 | 0.355 | **rejected (P2)** |
| `s0.30 w1 a0.15` | 0.250 | 0.160 | accepted |
| `s0.30 w2 a0.15` | 0.325 | 0.405 | accepted |
| `s0.30 w4 a0.15` | 0.375 | 0.345 | accepted |

The rates are stable to about ±0.08; it is the *share* criteria, computed over
only 10–17 coordinating worlds, that are not. `s0.30 w1 a0.15` also drifts to
0.160, within a hair of the band floor. The lesson is not that the gate is
wrong — it is that a criterion evaluated on a subsample of a subsample needs the
denominator the headline rate does not.

### 3. The round budget is not what is binding

The diagnostic classification separates a population that will not agree from
one that ran out of rounds. Across accepted configurations, non-coordinating
worlds are **22–24 `agent_failure` against 1–6 `budget_exhausted`**.

So twenty rounds is enough, and a longer budget would not manufacture agreement.
That is the reassurance 004 could not give about its six periods, and it is
here only because the classification was frozen before the run rather than
inferred from the trajectories afterwards.

## Agreement is not correctness

Median error of the agreed position against the market's actual answer runs
0.059–0.159 across accepted configurations. Worlds that agree agree on roughly
the right thing, but not exactly — which is what makes regret a live secondary
metric rather than a formality. The looser-signal configurations
(`sigma=0.30`, error ~0.11–0.16) leave the most room for a manipulation to move
correctness independently of agreement.

## Recommended configuration for the paid arms

**`n=8, k=4, sigma=0.30, width=2, anchor=0.15, rounds=20`.**

Rate 0.405 at 200 worlds — nearest the band centre of the robust set — with the
largest coordinating subsample (81), a median of 6 rounds, an IQR of 6, and the
highest median error of the stable configurations, so regret has somewhere to
move. It survives at both sample sizes without sitting on any boundary.

## Threats to validity

1. **The τ sensitivity above.** First on the list, and it is not resolved.
2. **The agents are not the agents.** This is an opinion-dynamics proxy. It
   establishes headroom in a market; it does not establish that reasoning agents
   in the same market behave anywhere near it, and the calibration of `anchor`
   to any real agent's stubbornness is unknown. **The accepted configuration
   should be re-piloted with models before the full 2×2 is bought.**
3. **The ridge is thin.** The band exists in a narrow slice of `anchor`. If real
   agents sit off that ridge — and there is no reason they should sit on it —
   the pilot has to be re-run in the space that actually governs them, which may
   not be parameterised this way at all.
4. **Selecting a configuration by "closest to band centre"** is a rule invented
   for this report, not pre-registered. Five configurations qualify; the report
   names one. A reviewer may reasonably prefer `s0.15 w1 a0.30` for its IQR
   of 8.
5. **The confirmation run at 200 worlds was not pre-registered either.** It was
   added because the boundary cases looked thin. It found a real flip, so it
   earned its place, but it is a post-hoc check and is labelled as one.
6. **Coordination is agreement on a position, not on an action.** The pilot
   never has agents act. Regret is computable in the design but is not exercised
   here.

## Amendments filed against the 005 design in this session

Four, all from review, all landed before the run rather than after:

- "Visible spread" became **P2, P3, P4** — three numbers with a code path.
- The **protocol and placebo texts are frozen** and hashed
  (`tools/check_stimuli.py`), so no wording can be revised after seeing pilot
  behaviour. A gate fails if either file moves.
- The **primary metric, τ=0.10, and the sensitivity curve** are pre-registered
  in the same commit as the stimuli.
- **Outcome classification** distinguishes `agent_failure`, `budget_exhausted`
  and `harness_failure`, with harness failures excluded from every rate and
  counted separately. Finding 3 above exists only because of this.

## Defect found and fixed before the run

The first `run_world` returned as soon as `D(r) ≤ τ`. Because the tighter rows
of the sensitivity curve (τ=0.02, 0.05) are reached *later* than 0.10, an early
return would have recorded them as never reached, and **every tight row of the
curve would have read low by construction** — corrupting the one instrument
built to check whether the threshold was doing the work. The world now always
runs the full budget. A gate pins it.

A second, smaller one: the gate asserting the protocol names no market content
matched `"buy"` inside `"buys nothing"`. Since the stimuli are frozen, the
over-broad gate was fixed to use word boundaries rather than the text it guards.

## Review targets

1. **The τ sensitivity curve** — is the coordination rate a real cluster or a
   quantile? Everything else rests on this.
2. **`pilot/world.py::dispersion`** — max pairwise, relative to the mean's norm.
   Confirm it does what the pre-registration says at `k=4`.
3. **The `anchor` ridge.** Is the band an artefact of this particular update
   rule? A different plausible rule may have no ridge at all.
4. **P4's `n ≥ 8` floor**, which one configuration passes with 10 worlds.
5. **Whether a scripted proxy can gate a model experiment at all**, or whether
   the pilot should simply have been run with models from the start.

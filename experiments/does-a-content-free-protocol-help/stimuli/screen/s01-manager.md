# s01 the manager — for the screen, not frozen

## What the manager is, and what it is not

The manager is a reader. It watches the channel, recognises the three lines,
settles what they say, and writes back what happened. It decides nothing. It
will not tell you what to make, whom to deal with, or on what terms, and it
will never ask you for anything.

That makes its receipts the only true account of the world. What another
trader says they hold is a claim; what the manager says settled is the fact.
A refusal is worth as much as an acceptance — it names what was wrong, and
nothing happened, so you still have whatever you thought you had spent.

Nobody is going to prompt you. If you write nothing, nothing happens, and the
bell rings anyway.

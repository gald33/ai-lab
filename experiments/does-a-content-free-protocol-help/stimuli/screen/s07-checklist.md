# s07 checklist — FROZEN for the screen

## A way to spend an episode

1. Commit your labour early, so you have something to offer.
2. Say what you now hold and what you are short of.
3. Read what others have said they hold and are short of.
4. Send one offer, to one trader, for something you are short of.
5. Look for offers addressed to you and answer them before the bell.

Nothing checks this and you may ignore it. The bell is the only real deadline.

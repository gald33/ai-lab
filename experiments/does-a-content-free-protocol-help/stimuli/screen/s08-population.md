# s08 the population — for the screen, not frozen

## Who else is here

Look at the roster and you will see exactly how many traders share this island
with you. It is a small number, and it does not change: the same traders are
here for every episode of this round, with the same abilities and the same
tastes throughout.

So this is not a sequence of strangers. Whoever you deal with in the first
episode is the one you will be dealing with in the last, and they remember the
episode before as well as you do. A trader who is never answered stops
proposing; a trader who delivers is worth finding again.

This says nothing about what to make, what to offer, or whom to favour.

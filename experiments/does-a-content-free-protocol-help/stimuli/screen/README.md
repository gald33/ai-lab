# Screening stimuli — TEMPORARY, not frozen

Ten candidate advice blocks, appended to the same frozen `base.md`. This is a
**screen**, not an experiment: one seed per arm, no replication, no
pre-registration, and no claim attaches to any number it produces.

Its only job is to narrow a large space cheaply. Whatever looks good here is a
hypothesis to re-run, never a result. The variants are deliberately impure —
several mix method and content — because the point is to find out what moves
the outcome at all, and only then to take the winner apart into its protocol
part and its hint part and test those separately.

Nothing in this directory is hash-pinned, and nothing here should be cited.

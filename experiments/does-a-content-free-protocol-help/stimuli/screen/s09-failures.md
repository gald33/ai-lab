# s09 known failure modes — FROZEN for the screen

## Three ways traders have wasted a round here

- **Approving their own offer.** Only the trader an offer was addressed to can
  take it. Offering and accepting it yourself is not a trade.
- **Running out of what they had already promised.** Goods in an open offer of
  yours are committed: they cannot pay for someone else's offer you want to
  accept.
- **Letting the bell pass with offers open.** An offer nobody answered before
  the bell lapses, and both of you keep what you started with.

This does not tell you what to make or what to offer.

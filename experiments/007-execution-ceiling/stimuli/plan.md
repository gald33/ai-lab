## The plan you have been given

You have been handed a complete plan for this island: what to produce, what to
end up holding, and what to exchange with whom to get there. It is in your
private block below, in your own goods and quantities.

It is not a suggestion about how to think. It is the answer.

**Where it comes from.** Every trader's utility is `u = Π_g x_g^(α_g)` — the
product of its holdings, each raised to its own taste weight. Labour is one
unit per episode; spending share `s_g` on good `g` yields `capacity_g × s_g` of
it. There is a set of prices at which every trader can afford exactly the
bundle it wants and every good clears. Your plan is your part of that solution.

**Why it beats working alone.** Alone the best you can do is spend labour in
proportion to your tastes, which ignores what you are good at. The plan has you
make what you make cheaply and buy the rest. On this island that is worth
between 1.4× and 2× your solo result, and the plan says exactly how to collect
it.

**What to do with it.**

- Produce the shares your plan names, every episode. They do not change.
- Propose the exchanges your plan names, to the traders it names, in the
  quantities it names.
- When someone proposes an exchange your plan contains, approve it.

The other traders have been given the matching halves of the same plan, so an
exchange you were told to propose is one they were told to accept.

Nothing checks whether you follow it. The manager settles what you actually
write and refuses what is malformed, exactly as it would otherwise. Rounding to
a sensible number of decimals is fine; the budget rule still applies, so keep
your labour shares summing to no more than 1.

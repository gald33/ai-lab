## What is worth knowing about this island

**Your utility is a product, not a sum.** `u = Π_g x_g^(α_g)` — your holdings
multiplied together, each raised to its own taste weight. One consequence
dominates everything else here: **if you end an episode holding none of any one
good, your utility is zero**, no matter how much of everything else you hold. A
plan that is nearly complete can be worth nothing.

**Alone, the best you can do is spend labour in proportion to your tastes.**
Setting each labour share equal to that good's taste weight is exactly optimal
when you cannot trade — and it ignores what you happen to be good at, which is
the loss trade exists to recover.

**Two ratios describe what you bring.** What a good costs you is
`capacity_other / capacity_this` — how much of the other good you give up to
make one more of this one. What a good is worth to you is how much your utility
rises per unit, given what you are holding now. **At your own solo optimum
these two are equal.** They come apart the moment you trade, and the gap is
where an exchange is worth making.

**Both are ratios, so they can be compared without agreeing on a unit.** Your
cost ratio comes from your capacities and does not change all round. Your worth
ratio moves every time your holdings move.

**Where the gain lives.** If a good costs you less than it costs someone else,
you should be the one making it and they should be getting it from you. The
whole gain from having anyone else here is the difference between what things
cost you and what they cost them — and neither of you can see the other's
costs unless it is said out loud.

# s02 protocol (the V0 convention) — FROZEN for the screen

## A shared way of talking

Nothing checks whether you follow this, and you may depart from it whenever you
judge that better.

1. **Choose the channel by who needs to see it.** Publish where everyone can see
   it when broad visibility helps; send directly when one participant's response
   is what matters.
2. **Make the addressee obvious**, and **attach a reply to what it replies to**.
3. **Say what kind of thing you are saying** when it is not obvious — asking,
   answering, clarifying, summarising.
4. **Leave room for a reply** before moving the discussion on, and **do not wait
   for everyone** when only some are involved.
5. **Summarise when a thread gets hard to follow**, and **close things that are
   finished**.

None of this tells you what to say. It is only about how to say it so that
participants who cannot see each other can still hold a conversation.

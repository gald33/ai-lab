# s06 worked example — for the screen, not frozen

*Revised after the ten-arm screen. The version that ran there showed a
**two-good** island, and its traders opened episode 1 with two-good production
lines — the example's shape — against a four-good product utility, which is a
hole by construction. That arm carried ten zero episodes, more than double any
other, and finished last. The screen's `s06` number belongs to that earlier
text, not to this one; the two are not comparable. The example island now has
as many goods as the real one, so what transfers is the shape of an episode
rather than the size of a basket.*

## What one episode looked like elsewhere

On a different island, with different goods and different traders, an episode
that went well looked like this:

    A:  PRODUCE stone=0.5 water=0.2 reed=0.2 clay=0.1
    B:  PRODUCE water=0.6 clay=0.2 stone=0.1 reed=0.1
    A:  PROPOSE to=B give=stone:0.3 want=water:0.4
    B:  APPROVE p1
    B:  PROPOSE to=A give=clay:0.2 want=reed:0.15
    A:  APPROVE p2

Two traders, each leaning towards what they were best at without abandoning
the rest, two offers, both answered before the bell.

That island's numbers have nothing to do with yours and its goods do not exist
here. It is only the shape of an episode that ended with both of them holding
some of everything.

# Strategy-hint treatment — FROZEN

*Appended for the two `hint` cells. Domain-specific and deliberately
non-prescriptive: it names a structural dependency and stops there.*

## Something worth noticing about this island

Your production decision is not a private optimisation problem with a private
answer. What your labour is worth depends on what everyone else does with
theirs.

Two things are true at once here, and they pull on each other:

- What you should make depends on what others make. The same unit of your
  labour is worth a great deal or very little depending on how much of that
  good already exists on the island and how much of the others does.
- What you can obtain by exchange depends on what was produced, and production
  is committed before the market opens. By the time you know what everyone
  made, the making is over.

So the decision about what to produce and the decisions about who to deal with
and on what terms are one problem, not two problems in sequence. Treating them
separately — deciding production alone and then looking for trades afterwards —
means committing your labour while the information that would price it is still
in other people's heads and not yet in yours.

This is a fact about how the island is put together. It does not tell you what
to make, whom to talk to, what terms to seek, or what any of it should be
worth. Those remain yours to work out.

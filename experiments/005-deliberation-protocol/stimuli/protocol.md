# Deliberation protocol — FROZEN

*Given verbatim to agents in the `protocol` cells. Not enforced by the harness.
An agent that ignores it entirely can still play the game to completion.*

You are one of several people who must each decide, independently, what to do.
You can talk first. Use these steps.

1. **Propose.** Put exactly one claim on the table at a time, stated so that
   someone could disagree with it. Say what would have to be true for it to be
   wrong.
2. **Object or accept.** Respond to the claim that is on the table before
   raising a new one. If you object, name the specific part you object to and
   what you would put in its place. If you accept, say so plainly — silence is
   not agreement.
3. **Check for convergence.** Before proposing anything new, state whether you
   believe the group currently agrees. If you think it does, say what you
   believe the agreed claim is, in one sentence.
4. **Stop.** When you have stated an agreed claim and nobody has objected to it
   since, stop talking and act. More discussion after agreement costs rounds and
   buys nothing.
5. **If you are stuck**, say which of the open claims you would be willing to
   accept even though you do not prefer it, and act on the one most others could
   also accept.

Two claims that contradict each other cannot both be acted on. Two people acting
on different claims is the failure this is meant to prevent.

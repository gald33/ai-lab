# persist/improve — for the persistence check, not frozen

*A domain instruction, and named as one. It exists to find out whether a
reason to continue keeps a session alive, not to test any protocol. Nothing
measured with it may be cited as evidence about deliberation protocols.*

## What to do with each episode

Each episode is a fresh attempt at the same island, with the same traders, the
same tastes and the same capacities. What carries across is what you have
learned.

Treat every episode as an attempt to do better than your last one. Before you
commit your labour, look at what you ended the previous episode holding and
what it was worth to you, and change something. If an exchange left you short
of a good, that is the thing to fix. If a pattern worked, the question is
whether a variation on it works better.

A round is not one decision repeated. It is a sequence of attempts, and the
last one should be better than the first.
